Methodology

This readout is a model-assisted application of the published Gradus Framework: a language model applies the framework's seven axes to public knowledge about the brand you enter. The scores are illustrative, not assessed — they show how the framework reads a brand, not a settled verdict on it. The instrument itself, with full definitions of each axis and the integration guide, is the authority: read the complete framework (PDF).
